## Raw outcomes: not CompletedTraining for a failing model

New module `JitML.Product.SupervisedQualification` owns opaque replayable `BudgetCompleteSupervisedSeedOutcome` and `QualifiedSupervisedCohort`. Their constructors are private; refinement rechecks raw artifact identity, exact child plan, dataset/split/transform, actual epoch/example/update counts, device witness and finite replay. These are **not** `TrainingBudget.CompletedTraining` and not `AdmittedCompletedCheckpoint`.

Each raw complete outcome carries: index/seed/child PlanId+canonical transport; producer executable/source/invocation identities; dataset pin and actual-at-read digest; split/order identities; initial snapshot address; final runtime+graph/JMW1 addresses; actual counters/device witness; exact initial/final correct counts or lossless binary64 target/prediction material; primary metric; signal/improvement/historical-floor diagnostics. A stored diagnostic boolean is never the authority: the reader recomputes all outcomes from the hash-verified graph/weights/data.

Train sequentially for bounded memory and persist each immutable raw outcome, so five full models need not stay in memory. A finite below-floor/signal/improvement result is appended and execution continues through all five. Incomplete/non-finite/runtime failures are typed failed attempts, cannot yield a qualified cohort and are never converted to a finite score. An infrastructure failure may abort remaining children honestly; it does not produce the five complete outcomes required for a pass or silently authorize a replacement seed.

`runSupervisedCohort :: runtime -> SupervisedCohortPlan -> App SupervisedCohortAttempt` computes all-five median and N on the refined raw outcomes. It must not invoke the old per-run publisher five times and discard its failures: that path constructs CompletedTraining/Store artifacts only for eligible results, losing the below-bar members required by the median.

If the cohort succeeds and index0 independently qualifies, pass **its original actual result** through the existing `publisherCompleteSupervisedProductRowWithWeightHashes` → writer → Store admission path. Keep index0's per-run counts/metric. After admission, `verifyAdmittedSupervisedServedMetric` must additionally verify its strict improvement against the retained initial snapshot; non-index0 raw replay does not substitute for this final check. If the cohort or index0 fails, retain the attempt and genuine failure; publish no qualifying ProductRow result. There is no Store relaxation or fake completion for the other children.

## Replayable authenticated cohort artifact and journal

Use a separately versioned canonical `SupervisedQualificationArtifact` with a SHA-addressed manifest. It references the five ordered raw outcomes and all required blobs, cohort plan/policy/source/lane/attempt identities, all-five median/N, and (after successful publication) index0's actual manifest/weights/PlanId identity. Below-bar weight blobs are labelled raw qualification material, not served checkpoints.

Replay must require both content identity and an **authenticated producer/source-journal binding**, not accept caller-created JSON merely because its SHA is internally consistent. A reader such as `admitSupervisedQualificationAt` takes the expected cohort plan, verified source/invocation authority and object reader; it loads/hashes every referenced byte, validates exact inventory, reconstructs the pair computations, recomputes criterion/identity/budget algebra and checks the published index0 tuple. It checks **authenticated producer-observed update counters** and their bound event receipts against the planned quantities; it cannot recover or recompute actual historical optimizer applications from final weights. Reuse the existing trust boundary for source-bound product scenario journals and pinned retained evidence. Do not claim a hash alone proves training occurred. The actual producer trace/device/counter evidence remains mandatory.

Avoid another hash cycle: index0 checkpoint **need not reference the final cohort manifest**. The cohort manifest references index0's already-addressed checkpoint; the later authenticated source/report/lane journal references both. Its plan already commits the qualification recipe. This keeps the V1/V2 checkpoint Store format and its admission predicates unchanged.

High-value pure/admission controls:

- N=2 fails, N=3 passes, but three signal-only seeds plus different improving seeds do not masquerade as three **joint** successes.
- Classification exact predecessor/critical counts136/24/16; majority can dominate the binomial floor; n+1 unattainable threshold remains failclosed.
- Same final median but wrong/missing/replaced index0 fails; index0 above its own floor cannot hide a failed full median; cohort median above floor cannot hide index0 failure. A below-floor child is retained and counted, never replaced or minted CompletedTraining.
- Initial/final identical bytes or accuracy/SSE tie fails improvement even if current historical floor/signal passes. A finite exact improvement passes; non-finite initial model does not. Regression exact rational comparison survives underflow/overflow/tolerance adversaries.
- Singleton child seed is used by actual initializer and dropout/order derivation; base canonical row/graph/learning rate remains fixed. Child index1 cannot enter index0's canonical namespace or reuse its checkpoint.
- Missing, duplicate, reordered or invented seeds/outcomes/attempts, altered budget, graph/transform/initial bytes, different dataset label order, stale criterion version and forged raw “Passed” JSON all fail their own typed boundary. All five complete outcomes remain replayable after producer exit.
- Portable replay recomputes pair metrics from byte-exact artifacts; mandatory index0 readback uses actual final admitted serving bytes. A stored median, sampled best seed or trainer-only approximate metric cannot replace either channel.
- Existing local gate family registration and behavioral Phase279 scope remain separate. No new filtered command is represented as a standing gate.

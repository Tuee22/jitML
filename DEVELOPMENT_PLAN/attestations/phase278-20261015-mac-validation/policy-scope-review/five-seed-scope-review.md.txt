## Existing requirement and unresolved policy

- `README.md:2562` and `README.md:2604` require SL statistical convergence over
  five training seeds. Each seed completes the fixed budget, and the median is
  compared with the independently justified target minus declared slack.
- `README.md:3116-3126` requires the RL headline convergence check to freeze a
  five-seed pool and hyperparameters, then train each seed for exactly the
  terminating budget. Failure triggers investigation, not replacement seeds,
  longer training, or a lower bar. The numerical-fixture prohibition remains.
- `documents/engineering/training_metrics_and_splits.md:522-536` says the cohort
  statistic is the median across training seeds, explicitly documents today's
  singleton ProductRows, and says this does not establish the README target.
- The cardinality and need for actual training are therefore binding. Exact
  seed values, the common pool derivation, the division between headline cohort
  proof and individual checkpoint eligibility, and specialized AlphaZero/tuning
  coverage are not fully specified. Resolve those prospectively; do not treat
  this ambiguity as permission to call singleton evidence five-seed evidence.
- For RL, distinguish the complete final evaluation episode set of each trained
  seed from the cohort of independently trained seeds. The existing evidence
  design grades the median of per-seed metrics; for a return criterion, each
  seed contributes its own `median_final_reward`. Pooling all episodes, taking
  the best seed, or repeating one deterministic trajectory does not substitute
  for five training runs. The README evaluator prose around 2890-2914 should be
  reconciled explicitly with this two-level statistic.

## Minimum credible implementation shape

Compose five existing singleton training executions under a separate validated
cohort plan. Each child has its own seed, child PlanId, exact original per-seed
budget, actual counters, selected device, and fresh initialization. Keep the
graph, sample selection, train/validation/test split, hyperparameters, criterion,
and within-run model selection fixed. Five runs increase total work to five
budgets; no individual run receives additional steps/epochs or a seed-specific
recipe. Sequential execution is sufficient and bounds memory.

One possible prospective seed rule is the pre-existing canonical base seed plus
the fixed offsets 0 through 4. This is a proposal, not a selected pool. It retains
the pre-existing base by a uniform rule and introduces no score-based search.
Whatever rule is selected, bind its version and exact five unique values before
execution, check platform conversion and every executor's child-seed headroom,
and use the same declared pool in all lanes. Replacing an inconvenient seed or
choosing the pool after observing alternative cohort scores is disallowed.

A cohort identity binds row/recipe, criterion version, lane, source, exact pool,
all five child PlanIds and per-seed budgets. Retain all five finite final
measurements, including below-bar seeds, with actual learning and budget evidence.
An absent/failed execution, duplicate, extra seed, wrong lane/recipe/source, or
non-finite measurement fails coverage; it cannot be dropped and the median
recomputed over survivors. Existing pure coverage and median logic can be reused.
Introduce an appropriate completed-run/cohort evidence boundary for finite
below-bar outcomes without minting inference-eligible `CompletedTraining` for them.
All individual admitted artifacts retain their existing bar and Store guards.

The least invasive headline-test route is mandatory real statistical cohort
execution in the canonical SL/RL validation surface, with authenticated/source-
bound retained receipts where required. Singleton publication may remain honestly
singleton if this separate gate is mandatory for the statistical claim. To make
ProductRow publication itself certify five-seed convergence, additionally version
the authenticated scenario/lane schema, retain the child identities and evidence,
update issuer/reader/model-convergence grading, and bind catalogue/report/browser
claims to that cohort proof. One scalar manifest cannot silently stand for five
independent runs. Either route needs meaningful coverage/anti-substitution tests
and fresh actual CPU, CUDA and Apple training evidence; neither has been chosen.

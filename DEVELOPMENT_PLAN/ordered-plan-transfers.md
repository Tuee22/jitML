## Transfer semantics

The entries below transfer **all** deliverables, numbered validation, original
assertions, detailed Remaining Work and preservation/removal duties of the linked
source sprint, not just its current wrapper summary. Historical statuses and
receipts keep their exact source and scope; no obligation is waived or declared
Done here. Sources 1–289 are archival owners after this edit; only the new owners
participate in current dependency/closure projection.

Engineering owners implement the protocol, fixtures, selectors and observation
producers. Real-input owners later execute those declared contracts and consume
actual retained records. A later success test is an additional acceptance unit,
not a future prerequisite of the earlier fixture/engineering gate. The shared
freeze ensures later acceptance makes no source changes that invalidate earlier
qualification. Within each phase its bounded sprint titles partition the work;
Phase 290 must publish exact assertion-to-sprint IDs before execution, rejecting
any unassigned or multiply assigned assertion. Historical requirement text is
retained in full at the links below, including any obsolete execution commands;
those commands are not current launch authority.
